The Codex — three aesthetic directions

Same app, same data, same flows. Three different visual systems for the cartographer-meets-grimoire feel you described, all using readable serifs (no decorative display fonts in body copy). Open any one fullscreen to click through the homepage, character cast, database browser and step-based generator. Pick a direction (or mix) and we'll go deep on the one you choose.

Deep ink-on-cream with red rubrications and ornamental fleurons. Cinzel small-caps for headings only; everything readable stays in EB Garamond. Drop-cap on the home title.
Direction C Field Notes — adventurer's journal
Open ↗
Cleaner, more modern but still warm and serif. Spectral throughout, field-teal & ochre accents, more whitespace, gentle rounded edges. The most "product-y" of the three.
